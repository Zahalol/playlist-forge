About Playlist Forge

Playlist Forge is a free, open-source hobby project. It turns the listening stats you export from Spotify into taste profiles, artist recommendations and ready-made playlists. There are no accounts, no fees and no ads.

How it works, in short

  1. You upload the Top Tracks, Top Artists and Top Genres text files that Skiley exports from your Spotify account.
  2. We look up each artist on Last.fm (genre tags, similar artists, listener counts, top tracks) and split your listening into profiles.
  3. Artists that listeners of your artists also play become recommendations, balanced across your profiles and dampened for very big names.
  4. Each profile gets a playlist as plain "Artist - Song" text, which Soundiiz or TuneMyMusic imports into Spotify for you.

Privacy

  • No Spotify login. Playlist Forge never asks for, sees or stores your Spotify credentials. You sign in to Spotify on Skiley (to export) and on the import tool (to save a playlist), both through Spotify's own login.
  • Your files and results are deleted after 24 hours. They live in a private job that only its link can open. They are never published, shared with other users, or written to the project's public code.
  • The shared cache holds facts about artists, not about you. Genre tags, similar artists, listener counts and top tracks are cached so that the next person is served faster. The cache does not record who asked.
  • Feedback is anonymous. A thumbs up or down is stored against the recommended artist, with no account, name or address attached.
  • Server logs hold request lines and counts only, no list contents. There is no analytics or advertising code.
  • Limits keep the free service free: files up to 100 KB, 12 files per upload, a handful of builds per hour per visitor, and jobs that expire after 24 hours.

Credits
