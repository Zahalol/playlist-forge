Get your listening stats out of Spotify

Playlist Forge reads the text files that Skiley exports from your Spotify account. The whole thing takes about three minutes.

Two sign-ins, neither of them here

You sign in to Spotify on Skiley to export your stats, and later on Soundiiz (or TuneMyMusic) to save a playlist. Both are established third-party tools that use Spotify's official login. Playlist Forge never asks for your Spotify login and never sees it: all it gets is the text files you upload, and it deletes them after 24 hours.

Step by step

  1. Open skiley.net and click Connect with Spotify. Spotify shows what Skiley may read; agree to continue. Skiley is free for this; you do not need Pro. {% if "01-skiley-login.jpg" in shots %}{% endif %}
  2. Open Stats in the left menu and pick a time range. The tabs are Last 4 weeks, Last 6 months and Last 12 months (ignore Last hours). Below the tabs are three sections: My top artists, My top songs and My top genres. {% if "02-skiley-lists.jpg" in shots %}{% endif %}
  3. Click Export to TXT file on each section. The button sits at the right of each section heading. Do it for artists, songs and genres, then switch to the next time range and repeat. Nine exports in all; the three 12-month ones are enough to start.
  4. Open My Tasks (bottom left) and download each file. Every export shows up as a finished task with a Download TXT button. Your browser saves files such as Top_Tracks_-_Skiley_Export.txt; repeats get a number added, like Top_Tracks_-_Skiley_Export__2_.txt. That is fine: Playlist Forge reads the contents, not the file name. {% if "03-skiley-export.jpg" in shots %}{% endif %}
  5. Upload the files here. Go to Start, select all the files at once, and check the time periods we detected. Then build.

Good to know

  • Skiley's free lists hold up to 50 items; a 4-week list can be shorter if you listened to fewer artists.
  • Only the text files leave Skiley. No playlists are changed, nothing is posted, nothing follows you.
  • If a file does not look like a Skiley export, the upload page says so and nothing is kept.
