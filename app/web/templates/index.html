Playlist Forge

Turn your Spotify listening stats into taste profiles, new music you will actually like, and ready-made playlists.

How it works

  1. Export your top tracks, artists and genres from Skiley as text files (last 4 weeks, 6 months and 12 months). Step-by-step guide.
  2. Drop the files here. We work out which file is which time period, so you do not need to rename anything.
  3. Get your listening split into profiles, with recommendations and a playlist for each.
  4. Paste a playlist into Soundiiz or TuneMyMusic to add it to Spotify.

You sign in to Spotify on Skiley (to export) and on the import tool (to save the playlist). Playlist Forge itself never asks for or sees your Spotify login.

Start

Free and open source. A first build takes about ten minutes while we look up your artists on Last.fm; your first profiles appear after a minute. Nothing is saved for longer than 24 hours. About and privacy.
