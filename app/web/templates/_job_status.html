Add a playlist to Spotify

Playlist Forge never sees your Spotify login. A free import tool moves the text into your account:

  1. Copy one playlist above (or download its .txt file).
  2. Open Soundiiz, sign in, and under Import playlist choose From text (free plan: 200 songs per playlist).
  3. Paste the lines, keep the format as "Artist - Title", give the playlist a name, and confirm.
  4. Choose Spotify as the destination and sign in to Spotify on Soundiiz. The playlist appears in your library within a minute; a few lines may not match and are listed by Soundiiz.

Alternative: TuneMyMusic (free plan: 500 songs per account in total), choose "Upload file" and the .txt download.
